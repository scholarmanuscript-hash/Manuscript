Privacy Policy & Terms

Commitment to Confidentiality and Academic Integrity

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Scholar's Manuscript, an initiative by ARCC. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us.

2. Confidentiality of Research Data

We understand that academic research, manuscripts, and dissertations contain highly sensitive and unpublished data. We adhere to strict non-disclosure protocols:

  • All submitted documents, datasets, and intellectual property remain the sole property of the client.
  • We do not share, sell, or distribute your work to any third-party organizations.
  • Files and data are securely deleted from our active servers upon completion and delivery of the final project, unless otherwise requested by the client for future reference.

3. Academic Integrity & Ethics

The Scholar's Manuscript operates under strict ethical guidelines. Our services are designed to assist researchers, faculties, and students in formatting, editing, statistical analysis, and structuring their work.

  • We do not promote academic dishonesty or ghostwriting that violates university regulations.
  • Our plagiarism reduction services involve legitimate paraphrasing and restructuring, but we do not guarantee publication in specific journals.
  • Final responsibility for the scientific content, claims, and ethical clearances rests with the author(s).

4. Data We Collect

When you use our inquiry forms, we may collect personal information such as your name, designation, institutional affiliation, email address, and phone number. This information is used strictly to communicate with you regarding your service request.

5. Revisions and Refunds

We strive for complete satisfaction. Revisions are provided based on the initial scope of work agreed upon. Refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis prior to the delivery of the final formatted or analyzed data. Once the final output is delivered and accepted, refund requests may not be entertained.

Questions about our Policies?
