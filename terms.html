Guidelines for Academic Excellence

1. Acceptance of Terms

By engaging with the services provided by The Scholar's Manuscript (an initiative by ARCC), you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions. These terms govern your use of our academic editing, drafting, data analysis, and printing services.

2. Scope of Services

We provide professional academic support designed to assist researchers, faculties, and students. Our services are intended for guidance, editing, and formatting purposes only.

  • Originality: We ensure that the content generated or edited by our team is free from plagiarism to the best of our ability, using industry-standard detection tools (e.g., Turnitin).
  • Academic Integrity: We do not write dissertations, theses, or assignments from scratch for students to submit as their own work. We provide assistance in structuring, data analysis, proofreading, and drafting based on the raw data/ideas provided by the client.
  • Publication Guarantee: While we prepare manuscripts to meet the standards of Scopus, UGC-Care, and SCI-indexed journals, we do not guarantee acceptance or publication by any specific journal, as this is solely the prerogative of the editorial board of the respective journal.

3. Client Responsibilities

To ensure the highest quality of service, clients are responsible for providing accurate, complete, and timely information, data, and feedback.

  • Clients must ensure they hold the copyright or have permission to use any data, images, or text provided to us.
  • Timely communication is essential for meeting deadlines. Delays in providing necessary information may affect delivery schedules.

4. Payment and Fees

Service fees are determined based on the scope, complexity, and urgency of the project.

  • A partial advance payment may be required before work commences.
  • Full payment is required upon completion and delivery of the final watermarked draft. The unwatermarked version will be released after full payment is received.
  • Prices are subject to change without prior notice, though ongoing projects will not be affected.

5. Revisions and Modifications

We are committed to delivering work that meets your expectations. Significant changes to the initial requirements may incur additional charges.
